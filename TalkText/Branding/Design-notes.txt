TalkText app icon

Source: AppIcon.icon (Apple Icon Composer 2 document).
Open: open -a 'Icon Composer' TalkText/Branding/AppIcon.icon
Regenerate exports from the repository root: TalkText/Branding/build-icons.sh
Requires Icon Composer 2 and an Xcode version supporting this document.

Five rounded vocal bars on the left and an outlined-as-fill I-beam on the
right represent speech becoming text. A cyan-to-indigo background supports
white foreground artwork. The same geometry carries through dark, clear,
and tinted appearances using Apple's automatic appearance treatment.

Artwork is vector SVG on an unmasked 1024-point square canvas. The gradient
is a native Composer fill. Foreground edges are crisp, with no baked-in
shadows, blurs, bevels, highlights, or enclosure shape. Composer supplies the
system mask, material, and lighting. Keep the mark's generous clear space
when making adjustments; its bars and cursor must survive small sizes.

Compiled/AppIcon.icns and Compiled/Assets.car are generated by Apple's
asset compiler for a macOS 14 minimum deployment target. Both are checked
in so SwiftPM bundle assembly and the development Xcode project can share
the same icon without upgrading the Xcode 16.4 release/CI toolchain.
After saving changes in Composer, rerun build-icons.sh and review previews.
Do not edit the compiled exports directly.
