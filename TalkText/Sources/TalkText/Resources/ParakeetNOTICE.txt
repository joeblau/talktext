TalkText uses Moondream Parakeet Ultra for speech recognition.
Original model: https://huggingface.co/moondream/parakeet-ultra
Parakeet Ultra is a full-precision post-training of NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3.
Base model: https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3
Model license: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
License terms: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode

Core ML conversion by FluidInference:
https://huggingface.co/FluidInference/parakeet-ultra-coreml
The conversion uses an int8 encoder and re-exported Ultra decoder and joint;
the preprocessor and vocabulary are from Parakeet v3.
TalkText uses the pinned conversion revision recorded in parakeet-model.json.

Inference SDK: FluidAudio 0.17.4, licensed under Apache License 2.0.
https://github.com/FluidInference/FluidAudio/tree/v0.17.4
The full SDK license and third-party notices are included in Resources/Licenses.
